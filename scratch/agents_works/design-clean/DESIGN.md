# Design Contract: North Roast Landing Page

## Winning Direction
**Modern Minimalist** – clean whitespace, generous padding, subtle gradients, focused typography, and a restrained accent palette.

## Design Tokens Used
- **Primary Accent**: `#533afd` (electric indigo) – used for CTA buttons and key highlights
- **Background**: `#ffffff` (clean white canvas)
- **Text**: `#1c1e54` (dark charcoal) for body copy, `#ffffff` for headings
- **Typography**: 
  - Display: `sohne-var` (custom font) – 56px title, 48px subtitle
  - Body: `sohne-var` – 16px regular, 14px secondary
- **Spacing**: 8px baseline grid, 16px padding between sections
- **Radius**: 8px on interactive elements

## Artifacts
- **Landing Page**: `landing-page.html`
- **Screenshot**: `landing-page.png`

## Rationale
The Modern Minimalist direction was chosen because it aligns with the coffee subscription brand's premium yet accessible identity. The stripe reference provided a strong foundation with its deep navy base, electric indigo accent, and restrained typographic hierarchy—all of which were preserved in the implementation.
